# Hive notes (prototype)

A phone web app for recording hive visits and getting what to expect and what to do next, from what you saw and the weather.

- Records and photos stay on the phone (localStorage + IndexedDB). Nothing is uploaded.
- Weather: Open-Meteo (only a yard's rounded location is sent).
- Install: open the site on your phone, then Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone) or ⋮ → Install app (Android).

Decision support from rules of thumb, not a vet or inspector.
