🐝 Hive notes

What to expect and what to do next, from what you see at the hive and the weather

Eric Hardy · MGT 409 · Nov 2026

The problem

  • US keepers lost 55.6% of colonies in 2024–25, the worst on record
  • Most are hobbyists with fewer than 25 hives, learning as they go
  • Losses come down to timing: feed before the freeze, count mites, catch a queenless nuc

“help newer people … figure out what the next step they should do in the hive” — a working beekeeper

What's new

  • Note apps log. Camera apps read one photo. Sensor apps need hardware.
  • Hive notes: your observations + live forecast + the colony's trend → a dated next step
  • Talk once for the whole apiary; AI answers checked before you see them
  • No hive hardware; records stay on your phone

Hosting, not tokens, is most of the cost at 10,000 users

Safety

  • Every Scribe field must quote the keeper
  • Advisor numbers must come from the records or tools; no doses; risks can't be dropped
  • Injection flagged and ignored; no tools that change anything
  • Daily $ cap, rate limit, access code, tool-round limit

8/8 server guards held · 4/4 live attacks blocked

Impact: a real keeper, a live hive

  • Frame Reader: 4 right / 1 wrong / 2 missed on 2 frames; ~$0.0005 a read
  • Closing taps confirmed: 4 of 4
  • Bugs: both frames lost on ›/Done; voice notes never heard a word → fixed that evening (v0.7)
  • “You need both your hands to do this” → talk once, after closing up

“I think the app is useful. I do like the fact that you can take the information in real time.”

Estimate: save one colony every two winters ≈ $90/yr per keeper vs. well under $1/yr of AI

What's next

  • 5–10 keepers through a club, a full season
  • Encrypted sync so mentors can see a newer keeper's records
  • Push reminders before freezes and strip-removal dates
  • A labelled frame-photo set to test or train the Frame Reader
