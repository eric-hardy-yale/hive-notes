🔍 Health is read by eye, one frame at a time.

Three agents. Nothing reaches the keeper unchecked.

🎙 Scribe turns speech into visit records. Every field must quote the keeper.

📷 Frame Reader pre-taps what it sees on a frame. The keeper confirms.

🧭 Advisor plans for one colony with live tools: forecast, history, treatment labels.

✅ Checker is plain code, not AI. Numbers must trace to records; no doses; no dropped risks. One retry, then the safe rules plan.

All on gpt-6-luna: fast, cheap, strict JSON[11].

Every answer is checked. Every test attack was blocked.[7]

  • Prompt injection → notes and photos are treated as data, never as instructions
  • Made-up facts → every record field quotes the keeper; every number traces to records or a tool
  • Harmful advice → no doses, labelled products only, high-risk warnings can't be dropped
  • Runaway spend → daily $ cap, rate limit, access code, at most 4 tool rounds

Field test: “I think the app is useful.”[8]

One working beekeeper, one live hive, 23 November.

  • AI frame reads: 4 right, 1 wrong, 2 missed (honey mistaken for brood), about $0.0005 each
  • Closing checks: the keeper agreed with 4 of 4
  • 2 bugs found at the hive, fixed that evening: lost frames, silent voice notes

“It kind of makes an unsafe process be a little bit more safe.”

If it saves one colony every two winters: about $80 a year per keeper (half a ~$165 package[4]), for under $1 a year of AI[6]. An estimate, not yet measured.

Next: hands-free, proven, and in more hives
