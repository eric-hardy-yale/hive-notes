🐝 Hive notes

What to expect and what to do next, from what you see at the hive and the weather

Eric Hardy · MGT 409 · Nov 2026

The problem

  • US keepers lost 55.6% of colonies in 2024–25, the worst on record
  • Most are hobbyists with fewer than 25 hives, learning as they go
  • Losses come down to timing: feed before the freeze, count mites, catch a queenless nuc

“help newer people … figure out what the next step they should do in the hive” — a working beekeeper

What's new

  • Note apps log. Camera apps read one photo. Sensor apps need hardware.
  • Hive notes: your observations + live forecast + the colony's trend → a dated next step
  • Talk once for the whole yard; AI answers checked before you see them
  • No hive hardware; records stay on your phone

Safety

  • Every Scribe field must quote the keeper
  • Advisor numbers must come from the records or tools; no doses; risks can't be dropped
  • Injection flagged and ignored; no tools that change anything
  • Daily $ cap, rate limit, access code, tool-round limit
